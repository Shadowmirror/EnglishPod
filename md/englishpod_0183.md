# Daily Life - Winter Clothes

**A:** Bye, mom!

**B:** Wait, Jimmy, it’s cold outside. Put a hat on!

**A:** Ok. Bye!

**B:** No, wait, you will be too cold without mittens.

**A:** Alright. See ya!

**B:** Hold on, with that wind, you’re going to catch a cold. Wear this scarf.

**A:** Ok, see you after school...

**B:** Oh... and ear muffs! Put these on... here we go.

**A:** Mom?

**B:** Yes, honey...

**A:** I... I can’t breathe.


## Key Vocabulary

- **mitten** *common noun, plural* — hand covering separat- ing thumb from fingers
- **hold on** *Interjection* — stop, wait
- **scarf** *common noun, singular* — a long narrow piece of cloth worn around the neck
- **ear muff** *common noun, plural* — a warm tubular cover- ing for the ears
- **honey** *common noun, singular* — name you call someone you love
- **hat** *common noun, singular* — head covering

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **snowsuit** *common noun, singular* — a one- or two-piece outer garment for cold weather, often consist- ing of heavily lined pants and jacket
- **gloves** *common noun, plural* — hand covering sepa- rating all fingers and thumb
- **coat** *common noun, singular* — an outer garment with sleeves, covering at least the upper part of the body
- **mitts** *common noun, plural* — short for mittens
- **boots** *common noun, plural* — a covering of leather, rubber, or the like, for the foot and all or part of the leg
