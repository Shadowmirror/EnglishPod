# Daily Life - Boxers and Briefs

**A:** Lily, I found a pair of men’s boxers in the laundry machine this morning!

**B:** What?! That’s weird. Are they your boyfriend’s?

**A:** Nah, Kevin only wears briefs. Plus, this pair is extra small!

**B:** What do they look like?

**A:** They’re light blue with thin pink stripes... Oh, and there’s a Snoopy on it which is hilarious, hahah...

**B:** Those are my undies!


## Key Vocabulary

- **boxers** *N* — loose-fitting under- shorts with an elastic waistband
- **laundry** *N* — clothes that need to be washed
- **briefs** *N* — close-fitting, legless un- derpants with an elastic waistband
- **hilarious** *A* — very amusing
- **undies** *N* — underwear (informal)

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **bra** *N* — a woman’s undergar- ment for supporting the breasts
- **iron** *N* — an appliance with a flat metal bottom, used when heated, as by electricity, to press or smooth clothes
- **maid** *N* — a female servant
- **washing machine** *N* — appliance for washing clothing
