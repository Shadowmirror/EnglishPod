# Daily Life - Mexican Food

**A:** Hello sir, welcome to Pistolera restaurant. May I take your order?

**B:** Yes, I would like the chicken cheese enchiladas with a side of guacamole.

**A:** I’m sorry sir, but we ran out of chicken. May I sug- gest our delicious beef burritos or cheese que- sadillas? Both include a side of guacamole and jalapenos.

**B:** Sure I’ll have the burrito.. Do you have nachos?

**A:** Of course sir. Our nachos come with melted cheese and chili.

**B:** Sounds good.

**A:** Would you like anything to drink?

**B:** Sure, I’ll have a Corona.


## Key Vocabulary

- **corona** *N* — a type of beer from mexico
- **jalapeno** *N* — a spicy green pepper
- **guacamole** *N* — a dip of mashed av- ocado mixed with tomato, onion, and seasonings
- **melt** *V* — to become liquefied by warmth or heat
- **burrito** *N* — a tortilla folded over a filling

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **taco** *N* — crisply fried tortilla folded over and filled
- **tortilla** *N* — a kind of thin pancake made from corn, cooked on a hot griddle until dry
- **dip** *N* — a liquid or soft sub- stance into which some- thing is dipped
- **Churro** *N* — a Spanish and Mexi- can pastry resembling a doughnut or cruller and made from deep- fried dough
