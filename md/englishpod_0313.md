# The Office - Small talk 3

## Key Vocabulary

- **long time no see** *P* — a greeting phrase used after two friends have not
- **same old same old** *P* — nothing new
- **promotion** *N* — new position
- **networking** *N* — meeting new people
- **split** *V* — leave here

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **demote** *V* — to reduce to a lower grade, rank, class, or position
- **depart** *V* — to go away,leave
- **blog** *N* — a journal written on- line and accessible to users of the internet
