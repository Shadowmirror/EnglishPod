# The Weekend - Planning A Crime

## Key Vocabulary

- **run through** *P* — rehearse quickly
- **snoop** *V* — pry into the private af- fairs of others
- **outta** *X* — out of
- **slammer** *N* — a prison or jail
- **intel** *N* — short for intelli- gence;information or instructions
- **lookout** *N* — person who watches and warns if there is danger
- **goahead** *N* — permission to do some- thing
- **rendezvous** *N* — a meeting planned at a certain time and place
- **hitch** *N* — mistake;an unforeseen obstacle
- **for Pete’s sake** *I* — a phrase used to express frustration

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **forgetful** *A* — someone who forgets details very easily
- **details** *N* — specific and important information
- **misdirection** *N* — to give the impression of one act but do an- other
- **unreliable** *A* — a person who cannot be trusted to do what is asked
