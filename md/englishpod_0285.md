# Daily Life - Handyman

**A:** The air conditioning is not working! We need to call a handyman before we start to fry in here!

**B:** Dan is on top of that. I think they are also getting the handyman to fix the bathroom toilet that keeps clogging up.

**A:** That would be convenient. They might as well ask him to fix the electrical wiring. The circuit breakers keep going out all the time. It’s really annoying!

**B:** Yeah you are right. This office is falling apart! Frank told me the other day that the gutters out- side were clogged and that’s why the parking lot was flooded.

**A:** I know! I was in ankle deep water trying to get to my car that day! The handyman definitely has his work cut out for him.


## Key Vocabulary

- **air conditioning** *N* — something that makes the air cooler
- **handyman** *N* — a person who is skillful at doing small jobs
- **on top of that** *P* — besides that
- **clog up** *P* — becomes blocked
- **wiring** *N* — the system of wires that carry electricity
- **fall apart** *P* — slowly break
- **gutter** *N* — long, hollow device that is attached to a roof

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **plumber** *N* — someone who installs the pipes, bathroom fixtures, and fixes toi- lets, sinks, etc.
- **electrician** *N* — someone who works with the electric wiring of a building
- **plunger** *N* — a rubber device that helps to unclog toilets
- **insulation** *N* — material used to regu- late temperature; keep a building warm in the winter and cool in the summer
- **gutter** *N* — a channel on the side of a building or street that guides away surface wa- ter
