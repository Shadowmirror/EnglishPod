## Key Vocabulary

- **to dash** *principle verb, present progressive* — go or run somewhere very quickly
- **o’er** *Contraction* — over
- **bell** *common noun, plural* — a hollow device that can ring when struck
- **make one’s spirit bright** *phrase* — cheer someone up
- **jingle** *principle verb, imperative* — make a gentle ringing noise

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Christmas** *common noun,* — Christmas song or
- **carol** *singular* — hymn
- **catchy** *Adjective* — pleasing and easily re- membered
- **alltime favorite** *phrase* — thought of as a favorite in its entire history
- **sleigh** *common noun,* — a journey in a sleigh
- **ride** *singular* — pulled by one or more horses
- **merry** *Adjective* — full of cheerfulness, fes- tive
