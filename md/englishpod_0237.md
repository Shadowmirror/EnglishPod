# Daily Life - Morning Routine

## Key Vocabulary

- **up and about** *P* — out of bed and begin- ning to move around
- **set aside** *P* — to keep time for a par- ticular purpose
- **frequently** *R* — often
- **seldom** *R* — almost never
- **at which time** *P* — whenever
- **from time to time** *P* — once in a while
- **oftentimes** *R* — frequently
- **makeup** *N* — cosmetics applied to improve your appear- ance
- **outfit** *N* — a set of clothes

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **curling iron** *N* — a tool for making straight hair curly
- **face wash** *N* — a soap used to clean the face
- **mouth wash** *N* — a liquid used to clear germs in bad breath in the mouth
- **french press** *N* — a specific technique to make coffee
- **body wash** *N* — liquid soap used to clean the body
