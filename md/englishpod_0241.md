# The Office - Interview Skills 3 - Education Back-

## Key Vocabulary

- **BA** *B* — bachelor of arts
- **major** *N* — the main subject stud- ied in a college
- **minor** *N* — a second subject stud- ied in a college
- **distinction** *N* — excellence
- **bookworm** *N* — a person who likes read- ing and studying
- **pay off** *P* — to effect positively
- **extracurricular** *A* — extra activities
- **keen on** *P* — willing or eager to do something
- **volunteer** *V* — want to do something without being forced or pay
- **involve** *V* — contain
- **fulfilling** *A* — full of happiness and satisfaction
- **push sb.** *V* — to encourage or per- suade (someone) to do something
