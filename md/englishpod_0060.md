## Key Vocabulary

- **drag** *Verb, Present Simple* — move something usually heavy
- **tie up** *Phrase* — to attach someone or something with a rope or string
- **fall for it** *Phrase* — believe a lie
- **gullible** *Adjective Phrase* — easily believing some- thing that is not true, easily fooled the truth is revealed,

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **wizard** *Noun (singular)* — the male form of a witch; man that prac- tices magic
- **spill the beans** *Phrase* — tell the truth when you don’t really want to; tell someone everything
- **disoriented** *Adjective Noun (singular) compulsive liar* — state of not knowing where you are, confused person that can’t tell the truth, lies about anything compulsive liar
- **frank** *Adjective* — used to say that some- one is speaking or writ- ing in a very direct and honest way
