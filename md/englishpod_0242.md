# Global View - Learning The Piano

## Key Vocabulary

- **tricky** *A* — requiring skill or cau- tion
- **scale** *N* — a series of musical notes
- **tempo** *N* — the speed at which a musical piece is played
- **by leaps and bounds** *P* — very quickly
- **musicianship** *N* — the skill of performing music
- **note** *N* — a specific musical tone
- **dynamics** *N* — changes in how loudly a piece of music is played
- **key** *N* — A button depressed with fingers to produce sounds

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **hammer** *N* — a wooden piece which strikes the strings
- **string** *N* — wires that when hit by the hammer produces sounds
- **plate** *N* — the iron frame which holds the strings in place
- **hitch pin** *N* — a metal screw that holds one end of the string
- **grand piano** *N* — a specific type of piano
