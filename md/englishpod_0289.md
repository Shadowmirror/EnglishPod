# The Office - Presentation Series 7 - Handling Tech-

## Key Vocabulary

- **kickoff** *V* — beginning of an event
- **recognize** *V* — Identify
- **crop up** *P* — appears or happens un- expectedly
- **rough round the edges** *A* — having a rough quality; not smooth or refined
- **bear with me** *P* — to be patient with me

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **kink** *N* — a flaw that hinders the success of an operation or plan
- **unprepared** *A* — without adequate preparation, not ready
- **stall** *V* — to intentionally delay
- **screwup** *N* — as a noun: a mess-up, can be used to refer to a person or thing; as a verb: to mess up
