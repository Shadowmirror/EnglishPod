# Elementary - Virus!

**A:** Oh great! This stupid computer froze again! That’s the third time today! Hey Samuel can you come take a look at my PC? It’s acting up again. It must have a virus or something.

**B:** Just give me a second, I’ll be right up.

**B:** I ran a virus scan on your computer and it turns out that you have a lot of infected files!

**A:** But I’m quite careful when I’m browsing the inter- net, I have no idea how I could have picked up a virus.

**B:** Well, you have to make sure that your anti-virus software is updated regularly; yours wasn’t up to date, that’s probably what was causing your prob- lems.

**A:** Ok. Anything else?

**B:** Yeah, try not to kick or hit the computer!

**A:** Um yeah... Sorry about that.


## Key Vocabulary

- **freeze** *Verb, Past Simple* — to stop working prop- erly
- **take a look** *Phrase* — see, revise
- **act up** *Verb, Present Progressive* — not working properly
- **be right up** *Phrase* — go upstairs soon
- **pick up** *Verb, Present Perfect* — get, acquire
- **browse** *Verb, Present Progressive* — look, search
- **up to date** *Phrase* — having all the most re- cent information

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **monitor** *Noun (singular)* — computer screen
- **computer tech** *Noun (singular)* — person in a company who fixes computers
- **corrupt file** *Noun (singular)* — damaged or broken computer file that can’t be opened
- **surf the net** *Phrase* — look for information on the internet
- **web browser** *Noun (singular)* — computer program that allows you to go on the internet
