# Daily Life - Buying A New Mobile Phone

**A:** Hello sir, may I help you?

**B:** Yeah, I accidentally dropped my phone in the toi- let.

**A:** I see. Well, you have come to the right place. We have over one hundred models of more than twenty leading mobile phone manufacturers.

**B:** Sounds good. I don’t want it to be too expensive, maybe something mid-range.

**A:** We have this new HTC smart phone. It comes with the Android OS so you can download appli- cations. It also has a built-in camera, mp3 player and touch screen. It works on the 3G network so you have fast access to the internet wherever you are.

**B:** What about Wi-fi?

**A:** Of course! You can access the internet from any hotspot as well as from home.

**B:** One last thing. Is it waterproof?


## Key Vocabulary

- **accidentally** *General Adverb* — by accident
- **lead** *Adjective* — advanced;fashionable
- **application** *common noun, plural* — software program
- **network** *common noun, singular* — a tele-communication system
- **hotspot** *common noun, singular* — place with internet con- nectivity
- **smart phone Android OS** *phrase* — a phone with wireless internet capacity management system

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **device Nokia** *common noun, singular* — instrument popular cell phone brand
- **SIM card** *common noun, singular* — ”Subscriber Identity Module;” card used in cell phones to place and receive calls, messages
- **iTunes app store** *phrase* — an online store cre- ated by Apple to sell applications for smart phones
- **to surf the web** *phrase* — to use the internet
