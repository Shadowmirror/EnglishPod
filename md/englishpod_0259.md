# The Office - Interview Skills 9 - Asking For Informa-

## Key Vocabulary

- **how about** *P* — what do you think
- **staff** *N* — employees
- **used to** *P* — accustomed or habitu- ated to
- **on call** *P* — to be available to work at any time
- **nine to five** *P* — 9AM-5PM

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **overtime** *N* — to work more than your normal working sched- ule
- **policy** *N* — a set of rules or proce- dures in a company
- **dress code** *N* — rules regarding how one should dress
- **pay slip** *N* — a receipt with informa- tion about your salary, deductions and taxes.
- **HR** *N* — Human Resources
