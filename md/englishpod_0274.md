# The Office - Presentation Series 2 - Talking about

## Key Vocabulary

- **amount to** *P* — to turn out to be
- **dramatically** *R* — in a sudden and ex- treme way
- **stand** *V* — represent
- **reap** *V* — to get something as a result of something
- **cutthroat** *A* — situation in which peo- ple compete in an un- pleasant way

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **pie chart** *N* — a circular chart divided into sectors, illustrating proportion
- **bar graph** *N* — a chart with rectangu- lar bars with lengths proportional to the val- ues that they represent.
- **line chart** *N* — a two-dimensional scat- terplot of ordered ob- servations where the observations are con- nected following their order.
- **market share** *N* — the proportion of in- dustry sales of a good or service that is con- trolled by a company.
- **sales forecast** *N* — the estimate of upcom- ing or future sales
