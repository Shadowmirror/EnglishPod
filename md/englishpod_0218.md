# Daily Life - Divorce

## Key Vocabulary

- **clean break** *V* — thorough or complete stop in a relationship
- **prenup** *N* — prenuptial agreement;a contract prior to mar- riage
- **custody** *N* — the act of keeping safe or guarding
- **concede** *V* — to acknowledge, often reluctantly, as being true
- **drag this out** *V* — proceed for an extended period of time
- **go our separate ways** *V* — set or keep apart in the relationship

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Bitter** *A* — to be angry
- **Sharing** *V* — To equally have posses- sion of something
- **Civil** *A* — to behave with class
- **Reconsider** *V* — To have second thoughts
- **Dispute** *N* — to have an arguement
