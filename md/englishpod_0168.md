# The Weekend - Cooking A Thanksgiving Dinner

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **mashed** *common noun,* — potatoes that are boiled
- **potatoes** *nonvariable* — and mashed, often with milk and butter
- **gravy** *common noun, singular* — brown sauce made from the fat and juices of cooking meat
- **stuffing** *common noun, nonvariable* — a mixture made of bread, herbs, etc. that has been stuffed inside a turkey and cooked with the turkey
- **apple** *common noun,* — an unfiltered, unsweet-
- **cider** *nonvariable* — ened, and non-alcoholic drink made of apple juice, sometimes with spices such as cinnamon
- **corn on** *common noun,* — corn still on the ear
- **the cob** *nonvariable* — that has been boiled or grilled
