# Global View - The Night Before Christmas

## Key Vocabulary

- **stir** *V* — to move,movement
- **hung** *V* — to fasten or attach (a thing) so that it is sup- ported only from above or at a point near its own top
- **chimney** *N* — a vertical structure that allows the passage of smoke
- **nestle** *V* — to lie close and snug
- **snug** *A* — warmly comfortable or cozy
- **nap** *V* — to sleep for a short time
- **clatter** *N* — noise
- **tore** *V* — rip
- **lustre** *N* — radiance of beauty, ex- cellence
- **sleigh** *N* — large
- **reindeer** *N* — large deer
- **rapid** *A* — with high speed
- **dash** *V* — to move with violence; rush
- **hurricane** *N* — very big rain and wind storm
- **obstacle** *N* — something that ob- structs or hinders progress.
- **courser** *N* — a thing suitable for run- ning or chasing
- **prance** *V* — to dance or move in a lively or spirited man- ner
- **paw** *V* — to strike or scrape with the paws or feet
- **hoof** *N* — an encasing protecting the foot of an animal such as horse
- **St Nicholas** *N* — another name for Santa clause
- **tarnish** *V* — to ruin, make dirty or destroy
- **soot** *N* — a black dirty substance
- **bundle** *N* — several objects or a quantity of material gathered or bound together
- **dimple** *N* — a small, natural hol- low area or crease seen when smiling
- **stump** *N* — the lower end of a tree or plant left after the main part falls or is cut off
- **pipe** *N* — smoking utensil
- **wreath** *N* — a circular band of flow- ers, foliage, or any orna- mental work, for adorn- ing the head or for any decorative purpose
- **plump** *A* — well filled out or rounded in form
- **elf** *N* — a magical being
- **thistle** *N* — a prickly plant

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **eggnog** *N* — a drink made of eggs, milk or cream, sugar, and, usually, rum or wine.
- **white christmas** *P* — having Christmas with snow
- **snowman** *N* — a figure of a person made of packed snow.
- **carol** *N* — a Christmas song or hymn.
