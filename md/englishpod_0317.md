# The Office - First Paycheck

## Key Vocabulary

- **stub** *N* — for keeping a record of the content of the part filled out and torn away.
- **federal** *A* — of or relating to a form of government or a country in which power is divided between one central and several re- gional governments
- **income** *N* — amount of money that you receive
- **fund** *N* — a sum of money or other resources whose princi- pal or interest is set aside for a specific ob- jective
- **selfexplanatory** *A* — needing no explana- tion,obvious

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **basic** *A* — elementary or simple
- **jargon** *N* — language peculiar to a particular trade, profes- sion, or group
- **welfare** *N* — financial or other assis- tance to an individual or family from a city, state, or national gov- ernment
- **deposit** *V* — to place for safekeeping or in trust
- **tax** *N* — a sum of money de- manded by a govern- ment for its support or for specific facilities or services
