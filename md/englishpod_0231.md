# Daily Life - Buying Women’s Shoes

## Key Vocabulary

- **support** *N* — something that holds a person
- **strappy sandals** *P* — a shoe with a bottom part having straps
- **edgy pump** *P* — a low-cut low-heeled shoe
- **opentoe shoe** *P* — the shoe with the toes exposed
- **twoinch heel** *P* — length of the heel is 2 inches
- **pointed shoe** *P* — the shoe having a nar- row top
- **pinch** *V* — squeeze between the thumb and a finger
- **insole** *N* — the inner sole of a shoe
- **cut into** *V* — tight and hurt the body with a sharp thing

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **snug** *A* — fitting closely
- **loose** *A* — not fitting closely or tightly
- **the (next) size up** *P* — one size bigger
- **the (next) size down** *P* — one size smaller
- **break in** *P* — to wear or use in order to make comfortable
