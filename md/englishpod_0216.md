# Daily Life - Health Food

## Key Vocabulary

- **eat right** *principle verb, indicative* — have healthy food
- **lifestyle** *common noun, singular* — the way people live
- **junk food** *common noun, nonvariable* — a high-calorie food and low in nutritional value
- **organic** *Adjective* — without using any chemicals
- **green** *Adjective* — healthy to the body
- **doughnut** *common noun, plural* — small ring-shaped fried- cakes

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Consciousness** *common noun, singular* — To be aware of some- thing
- **Health Nut** *phrase* — Someone concerned with living a healthy lifestyle
- **Enviromentalist** *common noun, singular* — Someone who is con- cerned with the enviro- ment
- **Support** *principle verb, present simple* — to be of help
- **Resolution** *common noun, singular* — The commitment to do something differently or begin to do something
