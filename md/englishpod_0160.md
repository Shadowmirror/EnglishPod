## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **prescription** *common noun, singular* — the medicine or remedy ordered by a physician for a patient
- **exclusion** *common noun, plural* — certain services that an insurance company will not cover in the plan
- **healthcare** *common noun, nonvariable* — the prevention and treatment of illness and the preservation of well-being through the services offered by the health professions
- **premium** *common noun, singular* — the amount paid for an insurance policy, often in installments
- **coinsurance** *common noun, nonvariable* — After the deductible is exceeded, it is when the insured must also pay a percentage of the to- tal cost of services while the insurance company pays the rest. It can be used instead of or in ad- dition to copays.
