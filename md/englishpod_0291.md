# Daily Life - Ordering Chinese Food

## Key Vocabulary

- **craving** *N* — a very strong desire for something
- **tempting** *A* — attractive
- **remedy** *N* — a medicine or treatment
- **takeout** *N* — food that is sold by a store to be eaten else- where
- **appetizing** *A* — delicious

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **adaptation** *N* — something that is changed/alerted to suit a new form
- **guilty pleasure** *N* — an indulgence,; some- thing that you eat, drink, or do that you shouldn’t, but makes you happy
- **hits the spot** *P* — just right; just what you were looking for (often refers to food or a drink)
