**B:** Sure! What’s playing?

Add in the fact that immortality and super human strengthis really sexy and there you have it! Plus the cast is hip, young people that make the movie even more enticing.

## Key Vocabulary

- **movie** *N* — movie
- **vampire** *N* — a dead person who sucks the blood of liv- ing person
- **tale** *N* — a story
- **fad** *N* — a temporary fashion or notion
- **werewolves** *N* — a wolf with extraordi- nary strength
- **zombie** *N* — a dead corpse, likes to eat brain
- **immortal** *A* — live forever
- **entice** *V* — to lead on by exciting hope or desire
- **hip** *A* — cool, fashionable
- **super human strength** *P* — extraordinary power

- **obsess** *V* — to think about some- thing unceasingly or persistently
