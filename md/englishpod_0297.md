# The Weekend - Ordering At An Italian Restaurant

**A:** Good evening ladies. My name is Josh and I’ll be your server tonight. May I take your order?

**B:** Do you have any recommendations?

**A:** Well, I personally like the chicken penne with cream mushroom sauce, but the prawn fettuccine is also very nice.

**B:** Hmm. I’d like to have the grilled chicken, but can I have spaghetti instead of penne?

**A:** Of course, mam. And for you?

**C:** I... ah..I’ll have the horse tripe.


## Key Vocabulary

- **server recommendationN** *N* — a waiter or waitress a suggestion or pro- posal
- **prawn** *N* — a large shrimp
- **fettuccine** *N* — pasta in the shape of long narrow strips
- **spaghetti** *N* — pasta dish originally from Italy
- **penne** *N* — a type of Italian pasta
- **horse tripe** *N* — the stomach of a horse used as food

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **parmesan** *N* — a type of cheese, usually goes with spaghetti
- **pizza** *N* — an Italian dish, round in shape
- **calzone** *N* — a turnover made of pizza dough, usually containing cheese
- **ricotta** *N* — a soft Italian cheese that resembles cottage cheese.
