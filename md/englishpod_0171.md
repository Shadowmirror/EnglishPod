## Key Vocabulary

- **pointandshoot** *Adjective* — Any compact non-SLR camera with non- interchangeable lenses, using automatic set- tings and simplified controls. Generally desired by novices and casual users.
- **interest someone in** *phrase* — try to persuade some- one to buy, do, or eat something
- **lens** *common noun, singular* — a transparent optical device used to converge or diverge transmitted light and to form im- ages
- **polarizing filter** *phrase* — a camera lens filter used to control the plane of polarization of light en- tering the lens.
- **entrylevel** *Adjective* — simple and appropri- ate to an inexperienced person

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **tripod** *common noun, singular* — a three-legged stand for a camera
- **filter** *common noun, singular* — a lens screen of dyed gelatin or glass for con- trolling color or for di- minishing the intensity of light
- **exposure** *common noun, singular* — the process of light hit- ting a photosensitive surface, such as film or a digital image sensor
- **shutter** *common noun, singular* — a mechanical device for opening and closing the aperture of a camera lens to expose film
- **wide** *common noun,* — a lens which has a wide
- **angle lens** *singular* — coverage angle so it can take in a great deal of scene, usually any lens shorter than 42mm
