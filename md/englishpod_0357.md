# Daily Life - Making A Dinner Reservation

**A:** Bruno Bistro, how may I help you?

**B:** Yes hello, I would like to make a reservation please.

**A:** Certainly sir, For which day and time please?

**B:** Tonight at seven.

**A:** I’m sorry sir, but we are fully booked tonight until eight.

**B:** In that case, eight o’clock is fine.

**A:** Very well, and how many people will attend tonight?

**B:** Four people.

**A:** Lastly, may I please know what name I should make the reservation under?

**A:** Mark.


## Key Vocabulary

- **bistro** *N* — mid-priced restaurant
- **make a reservation** *P* — to make an arrange- ment to hold something for later
- **fully booked** *P* — all sold out or full of reservations
- **in that case** *P* — if something said earlier is true
- **attend** *V* — go to
- **reservation** *N* — Something that is kept back or withheld

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **steakhouse** *N* — a restaurant that spe- cializes in beef steaks.
- **diner** *N* — A place that offers a wide range of foods, mostly American, a ca- sual atmosphere and late operating hours.
- **Soup Kitchen** *N* — a place where food is of- fered to the hungry for free or at a reasonably low price
- **saltshaker** *N* — a container with a per- forated top for sprin- kling salt
- **Cutlery** *N* — Utensils such as knives, forks, and spoons used as tableware
