## Key Vocabulary

- **infant** *common noun, singular* — baby
- **quake at the sight** *phrase* — shake or tremble when see something
- **afar** *General Adverb* — from or to a great dis- tance
- **saviour** *common noun, singular* — a person who rescues people from harm or danger
- **thy** *Adjective* — an old-fashioned, po- etic, or religious word for ’your’

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Magi** *proper noun, plural* — the wise men, generally assumed to be three in number, who travelled to the infant Jesus to worship him
- **proclamation** *common noun, singular* — a public and official an- nouncement
- **stable** *common noun, singular* — a building for the shel- ter and feeding of do- mestic animals, espe- cially horses and cattle
- **manger** *common noun, singular* — a box or trough in a sta- ble or barn from which horses or cattle eat
- **nativity** *common noun,* — a depiction of Jesus’s
- **scene** *singular* — birth in a manger, including the parents, Mary and Joseph
