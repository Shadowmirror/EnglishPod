# Daily Life - Facial Hair

## Key Vocabulary

- **facial hair** *P* — hair on the face
- **beard** *N* — the hair growing on the lower part of a man’s face
- **mustache** *N* — an unshaved growth of hair on the upper lip
- **goatee** *N* — a small chin beard trimmed into a point
- **sideburns** *N* — hair that grows on the sides of man
- **thick** *A* — not thin;great in extent from the surface
- **ridiculous** *A* — very unreasonable or silly
- **why yes** *P* — as a matter of fact

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Sketch Artist** *N* — A person who can draw a face from instruction
- **razor** *N* — a sharp instrument used to shave a beard
- **Shaving cream** *N* — a lather applied to the face for a smooth shave
- **Hair Clippers** *N* — An electric machine that cuts hair
- **Muttonchops** *N* — Sideburns that are long and extend towards the corner of the mouth
