## Key Vocabulary

- **possess** *verb* — have or own
- **survival** *common noun, nonvariable* — the state of living and being alive
- **defend** *verb* — fight in order to keep something or someone safe
- **rough** *phrase* — be tough,difficult, vio- lent
- **no matter** *common* — even if it’s difficult
- **what** *noun, singular*
- **get down** *phrase* — lower your body, not standing
- **stand in someone’s way** *phrase* — prevent a person from doing something
- **at all costs** *phrase* — regardless of the cost involved, even if it’s dif- ficult
