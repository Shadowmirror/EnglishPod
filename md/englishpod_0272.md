# Daily Life - Getting A Nanny

## Key Vocabulary

- **exhausted** *A* — very tired
- **hire** *V* — to give work
- **nanny** *N* — a woman who is paid to care for a young child
- **chore** *N* — a small job that is done regularly
- **errand** *N* — short journey that you take to do or get some- thing
- **juggle** *V* — to do several things at the same time
- **refer** *V* — to send someone to a person or place for treatment

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Babysitter** *N* — A person that looks af- ter kids when their par- ents are not home
- **Domestic Help** *N* — Any person that is hired to work at a resi- dence
- **Minimum Wage** *N* — The lowest legal wage a person can earn either hourly or monthly
- **Maid** *N* — A woman that helps with the household chores such as cleaning and cooking
- **Butler** *N* — A man that helps with the household chores such as cleaning and cooking
