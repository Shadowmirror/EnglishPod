# The Weekend - Asking For Directions

**A:** We have been going around in circles for the past hour! Will you just please stop and ask for direc- tions?

**B:** We are not lost! I’m just taking the scenic route.

**A:** Yeah, whatever. I told you we should buy that GPS that was on sale, but would you listen to me? No! This is so typical.

**B:** Fine! I’ll ask this guy for directions if it will shut your trap! Excuse me, sir. Can you tell me how to get to Saint Mary’s Church?

**C:** Sure! Go down Park Road. turn left, go up as far as the set of traffic lights and turn left. The place you are looking for isthe second building on the right.

**A:** Thanks!

**B:** See? Was that hard? If you would only listen to me more often, you would be better off.


## Key Vocabulary

- **typical** *Adjective* — having the usual char- acteristics (of)
- **direction** *common noun, plural* — an instruction leading to a place or point
- **on sale** *phrase* — special offer or discount
- **get to** *principle verb, infinitive* — reach;be able to arrive at(in)
- **better off** *phrase* — be in a better condition
- **go** *principle verb,* — circulate
- **around GPS** *present perfect progressive* — Global Positioning Sys- tem
- **shut your trap** *phrase* — shut up

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **freeway** *common noun, singular* — A highway without tolls. Also known as an expressway
- **Toll** *common noun, singular* — A fixed charge or tax for a privilege, espe- cially for passage across a bridge or along a road.
- **Pedestrian** *common noun, singular* — a person travelling on foot; walker
- **middle of nowhere** *phrase* — a remote or far away place where there are very few people or buildings
- **Hitchhiker** *common noun, singular* — A person that travels by soliciting free rides along a road.
