# The Office - Sending A Quote Via Email

## Key Vocabulary

- **costing** *V* — charge, make someone pay
- **please note that** *N* — being informed of some- thing
- **quotation** *N* — a statement of the price of an item or service
- **play it safe** *P* — go with the safest op- tion
- **I am writing to** *N* — introduction

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **cargo** *N* — freight of a ship, air- plane
- **link** *N* — a word icon, etc, on which a user may click to move to another part of or different web- site,document
- **attachment** *N* — omething attached, as a document added to a letter, or an picture in a email etc
- **etiquette** *N* — conventional require- ments as to social behavior; proprieties of conduct as estab- lished in any class or community or for any occasion.
