# Daily Life - New Furniture

**A:** How about this floor lamp?

**B:** Fine just get it! We have been shopping for furni- ture for five hours! I’m so tired!

**A:** We still need to find an armoire and a dresser.

**B:** Fine! I am going to go home and drop off this nightstand, coffee table and love seat while you look for the rest of the things.

**A:** Great! Pick me up in about an hour because I think I’ll also get a bean bag and a dining set.

**B:** While you are at it can you pick out a nice re- cliner? I really want one so I can watch TV.

**A:** Recliner? In my beautifully decorated living room? I don’t think so!


## Key Vocabulary

- **floor lamp** *P* — a tall lamp with a base that stands on the floor
- **armoire** *N* — a large wardrobe or cabinet
- **dresser** *N* — a set of shelves usually used for holding clothes
- **nightstand** *N* — a small table or stand placed at a bedside
- **love seat** *N* — small sofa that seats two people
- **recliner** *N* — an armchair that can be reclined
- **while you are at it** *P* — during the time you are there

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Sectional** *N* — one large couch with different sections
- **coffee table** *N* — A table that is set in the middle of a living room
- **bar stools** *N* — Chairs that are placed at a bar
- **family room** *N* — the room where the family spends time to- gether
