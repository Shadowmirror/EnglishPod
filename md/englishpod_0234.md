# Daily Life - Going to the Doctor

## Key Vocabulary

- **swollen** *A* — some parts are bigger than others due to a certain
- **come down with** *P* — get sick
- **antibiotics** *N* — chemical substance that can kill a virus
- **contagious** *A* — having a sickness that other people can catch
- **a doctor’s note** *P* — note from doctor ex- plaining a patient’s ab- sence due to sickness
- **dehydrated** *V* — not having enough wa- ter in the body
- **breathless** *A* — unable to take enough air into your lungs

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **injection** *N* — a liquid injected into the body for medicinal purposes by use of a needle
- **waiting room** *N* — a room for people to wait before going in to see the doctor
- **stethoscope** *N* — instrument used for lis- tening to sounds pro- duced within the body, such as the heartbeat
- **otoscope** *N* — instrument for exam- ining the interior of the ear, especially the eardrum, consisting es- sentially of a magnify- ing lens and a light
- **examining (exam) room** *N* — a small room where a physician may examine a patient with some pri- vacy
