# The Office - Interview Skills 4 - Talking About Work

## Key Vocabulary

- **end up** *P* — turned out or result in
- **in charge of** *P* — be responsible for
- **manage to** *V* — be able to
- **step down** *P* — a decrease or reduction in size or amount
- **prospect** *N* — a mental picture of something to come

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **priority** *N* — to place importance over different objec- tives
- **career** *N* — the specific area of work a person pursues
- **quit** *V* — to stop doing an activ- ity
- **unemployed** *A* — to not have a job
- **employable** *A* — the ability to be em- ployed
