# Global View - Physics

## Key Vocabulary

- **encompass** *V* — to include (something) as a part
- **conceptual** *A* — based on or relating to ideas or concepts
- **grasp** *N* — an understanding of something
- **black hole** *N* — an invisible area in outer space
- **quantum mechanics** *N* — the branch of physics that deals with en- ergy and matter on an atomic level
- **hypothetical thermodynamicsN electromagnetismN** *A* — involving or based on a suggested idea or the- ory a science that deals with the action of heat a magnetic field that is produced by a current of electricity
- **nuclear physics** *N* — the area of physics which deals with nuclei

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **academia** *N* — the various interests of a university and those who teach there
- **applicable** *A* — something that can be used in real-life situa- tions
- **gravity** *N* — the force of attraction between two objects
- **ivory tower** *N* — refers to those who live in intellectual isolation, often without contact or applicability to the outside world
- **relevant** *A* — knowledge that can be applied to everyday sit- uations
