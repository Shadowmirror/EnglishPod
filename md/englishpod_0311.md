# The Office - Sorry I’m Late

## Key Vocabulary

- **hang on a sec** *P* — hold on
- **wait ages** *V* — wait for a very long time
- **hold up** *V* — stuck
- **I am terribly sorry** *P* — Very sorry
- **please excuse me** *R* — pardon

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **late** *A* — occurring, coming, or being after the usual or proper time
- **absent** *A* — not in a certain place at a given time
- **excuse** *V* — to offer an apology for; seek to remove the blame of
- **slack** *A* — negligent; careless
