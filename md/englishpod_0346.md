# Global View - All About Wines

## Key Vocabulary

- **notoriously** *A* — publicly or generally known, as for a partic- ular trait
- **aroma** *N* — the way something smells
- **crisp** *A* — invigorating
- **bonedry** *A* — no moisture, dry
- **subtle** *A* — Fine or delicate

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **chardonnay** *N* — a dry white wine made from this grape
- **grape vine** *N* — A vine on which grapes grow.
- **acidity** *N* — The state, quality, or degree of being acid.
- **zesty** *A* — affecting the organs of taste or smell with a strong and often harsh sensation
- **sharp** *A* — quick, acute, or pene- trating
