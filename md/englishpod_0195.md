# Global View - Job Hunting

**A:** Woo hoo! This just might be the start of the rest of my life!

**B:** What happened?

**A:** I’m in the market for a job! I went on a website with hundreds of job listings in the area and browsed through them until I got the names of a few em- ployers I would like to work for. I have the resume I wrote for English class last month and a cover letter will be a piece of cake to write. I’ve even done my research and found the names of the managers so I can address the letters personally. And you know I can be charming in interviews. Goodbye my penniless days! Hello salary and a career!

**B:** Ben, we’re fifteen. What kind of job are you look- ing for?

**A:** Oh, just for a position as a gas station attendant. You know, starting at a simple lowly job, just like all the greats before they made it big in the world.

**B:** Uh-huh.

**A:** But I’m just in it for the money, right? How else am I going to be able to afford to keep taking An- gela to the movies? Besides, I love the smell of gasoline, don’t you?


## Key Vocabulary

- **manager** *common noun, plural* — person who manages an organization or com- pany
- **charming** *Adjective* — delightful; pleasant; at- tractive
- **interview** *common noun, plural* — a conversation with or questioning of a person
- **penniless** *Adjective* — very poor; almost to- tally without money
- **salary** *common noun, singular* — money paid by the em- ployer each month
- **career** *common noun, singular* — profession that some- one does for a long time
- **position** *common noun, singular* — job, occupation
- **gas sta** *common noun,* — place where gasoline is
- **tion** *singular* — sold
- **attendant** *common noun, singular* — a person employed to provide a service
- **lowly** *Adjective* — humble or low in posi- tion
- **greats** *common noun, plural* — person with importance or distinction in their field
- **uhhuh** *Interjection* — a less emphatic variant of yes
- **gasoline** *common noun, nonvariable* — petroleum fuel
- **browse** *principle verb, past simple* — to search for and read hypertext
- **employer** *common noun, plural* — a person or firm who employs
- **resume** *common noun, singular* — a summary of your aca- demic and work history
- **address** *principle verb, present simple* — to direct a speech or written statement to someone
- **job listing** *phrase* — database containing an ordered array of job items
- **a piece of cake** *phrase* — something is easily to be done
- **make it big** *phrase* — make a great success
- **in the market** *phrase* — be interested in buying
- **woo hoo** *Interjection* — exclamation of excite- ment

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Headhunter** *common noun, singular* — an employment re- cruiter, sometimes referred to as executive search.
- **Job** *common noun,* — A company that helps
- **Agency** *singular* — both employers and job applicants find a posi- tion.
- **CV** *common noun, singular* — Curriculum Vitae; a summary of academic and professional history and achievements
- **No Brainer** *phrase* — something that is easy or not challenging
- **Easy As Pie** *phrase* — something that is easy or not challenging
