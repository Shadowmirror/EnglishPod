# The Office - Interview Skills 8 - Discussing Salary

## Key Vocabulary

- **remuneration package** *N* — money and benefits given to someone for the work
- **per annum** *P* — each year
- **housing allowance** *A* — money that a company provides for rent of a house
- **flexible** *A* — able to change or to do different things
- **go through** *V* — to study or look at (something) in a careful way

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Gross** *A* — Overall sum excluding tax and any deductions
- **Net** *A* — Free from all charges or deductions
- **Income Tax** *N* — A tax on the amount of money you earn
- **Stock Options** *P* — A benefit offered by a company to purchase stock for a fixed price
- **Bonus** *N* — An additional payment or reward given for good work or perfor- mance
