# Daily Life - Talking To Your Roomate

**A:** Charlie, do you have a second?

**B:** Yeah what’s up?

**A:** Well, I went and paid the bills today and you still haven’t given me your half.

**B:** Yeah I wanted to talk to you about that. I agreed we would go halves on allthe bills, but frankly I think it’s unfair.

**A:** Unfair! Why?

**B:** Well, you have long hair and use the hairdryer ev- ery morning. I don’t. You leave your computer on all night downloading torrents. I don’t. You see what I’m getting at here?

**A:** You leave the air conditioner on day and night! You also take 30 minute showers which means you are using way more gas and water than me!

**B:** Well, while we are at it, stop bringing your friends over for drinks every weekend. You always leave a mess and keep me up all night!

**A:** Maybe you should just move out and find another place.

**B:** Maybe you should move out!


## Key Vocabulary

- **bill** *common noun, plural* — a document that shows how much you must pay
- **go halves** *phrase* — divide equally
- **move out** *phrase* — to vacate a residence, cause to leave
- **what I am getting at** *phrase* — what I am trying to say
- **way more** *phrase* — much more, a lot more

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Landlord** *common noun, singular* — One that owns and rents land, buildings, or dwelling units.
- **Lease** *common noun, singular* — A contract granting use or occupation of prop- erty during a specified period in exchange for a specified rent.
- **Utility** *common noun, singular* — A commodity or ser- vice, such as electricity, water, or public trans- portation, that is pro- vided by a public utility
- **Dower** *common noun, singular* — The rights of a widow in the property of her hus- band at his death.
- **Breach Of Contract** *phrase* — A legal violation of an established contract be- tween two parties
