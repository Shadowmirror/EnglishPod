# The Weekend - Decorating a Christmas Tree

## Key Vocabulary

- **huge** *Adjective* — very big
- **ornament** *common noun, plural* — decoration, something used to beautify
- **decorate** *principle verb, present simple* — beautify
- **of course** *phrase* — certainly true
- **stocking** *common noun, plural* — large sock; traditionally Christmas gifts are put inside

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **tinsel** *common noun, nonvariable* — very thin strips of shiny metallic material
- **garland** *common noun, singular* — a chain of flowers, leaves, or tinsel
- **wreath** *common noun, singular* — a closed circle of gar- land
- **light up the Christmas tree** *phrase* — turn on the Christmas tree lights
- **Christmas** *common noun,* — a large ornament in the
- **star/angel** *singular* — shape of a star or an an- gel, usually with lights, placed on the very top of the Christmas tree
