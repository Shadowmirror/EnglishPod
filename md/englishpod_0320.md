# Global View - Taking The GMAT

## Key Vocabulary

- **corporate** *A* — related to business
- **management** *N* — the action of controlling an area of a business
- **graduate** *N* — person who finished university
- **GMAT** *N* — Graduate Management Admissions Test
- **analytical** *A* — pertaining to or pro- ceeding by analysis
- **quantitative** *A* — type of information based in quantities
- **verbal** *A* — consisting of or in the form of words
- **MBA** *N* — Master of Business Ad- ministration
- **fat cat** *P* — A wealthy and privi- leged person

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **greed** *N* — excessive desire, as for wealth or power
- **wizard** *N* — a person of amazing skill or accomplishment
- **market** *N* — the field of trade or business
- **thesis** *N* — a dissertation on a par- ticular subject in which one has done original research
- **professor** *N* — a teacher of the highest academic rank in a col- lege or university
