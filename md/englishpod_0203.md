# Daily Life - Calling Tech Support

## Key Vocabulary

- **oh man** *Interjection* — to express emotion or feeling, like God, Jesus
- **calm down** *phrase* — become quiet or calm, relax
- **laptop** *common noun, singular* — a small computer which is easily carried
- **I mean** *phrase* — to express ones own opinion, meaning
- **monitor** *common noun, singular* — an electronic display that shows images
- **virus** *common noun, plural* — a program designed to harm a computer
- **nope** *General Adverb* — no (informal)
- **program** *common noun, singular* — a set of instructions to perform
- **crash** *principle verb, past simple* — stop working or func- tioning properly
- **antivirus** *common noun, plural* — a program designed to identify and remove computer viruses
- **run** *principle verb, imperative* — to work or be in opera- tion
- **spyware** *common noun, singular* — software that secretly gathers information

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **Hotline** *common noun, singular* — A telephone line that gives quick and direct access to a source of in- formation or help
- **Phisihing** *common noun, singular* — To request confidential information over the In- ternet under false pre- tenses in order to fraud- ulently obtain credit card numbers, pass- words, or other per- sonal data
- **Crack** *verb* — To break open or into something such as a safe or a computer sys- tem
- **Format** *verb* — To divide (a disk) into marked sectors so that it may store data.
- **Hardware** *common noun, singular* — A computer and the as- sociated physical equip- ment directly involved in the performance of data-processing or com- munications functions.
