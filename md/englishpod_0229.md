# Daily Life - Buying Men’s Shoes

## Key Vocabulary

- **dress shoe** *N* — a shoe used as a compo- nent of formal wear
- **tennis shoe** *N* — a canvas shoe with a pliable rubber sole
- **tongue** *N* — the flap of material un- der the laces of a shoe
- **grip** *N* — way of grasping and holding firmly
- **shoelaces** *N* — a long, thin material used for fastening a shoe
- **sole** *N* — the underside of footwear

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **basketball sneakers** *N* — sneakers worn specifi- cally to play basketball
- **cross trainers** *N* — shoes worn for running or other physical activ- ities
- **arch supports** *N* — a pad used to give the feet more support
- **shoe salesmen** *N* — a person who sells shoes
- **construction boots** *N* — hard and durable boots worn for construction
