## Key Vocabulary

- **All Soul’s Day** — November 2 observed in some Christian churches as the day of the dead
- **among** *Preposition* — among
- **honor the departed** *principle verb, present progressive* — confer honor on the people who have passed away

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **pass away** *phrase* — to die, to perish
- **burial** *common noun, singular* — the act of burying, esp the interment of a dead body
- **wake** *common noun, singular* — A watch over the body of a deceased person be- fore burial, sometimes accompanied by festiv- ity. Also called region- ally viewing.
- **funeral** *common noun,* — A group of persons,
- **procession** *singular* — vehicles, or objects moving along a casket through the streets
- **cremate** *verb* — to burn up (something, esp a corpse) and re- duce to ash
