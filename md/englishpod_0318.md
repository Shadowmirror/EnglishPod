# Global View - Allergies

## Key Vocabulary

- **pollen** *N* — the fertilizing element of flowering plants, con- sisting of fine, pow- dery, yellowish grains or spores
- **desensitize** *V* — to lessen the sensitive- ness of
- **allergic** *A* — have allergy towards something
- **affect** *V* — to act on; produce an effect or change in
- **dust** *N* — dry fine powdery mate- rial, such as particles of dirt, earth or pollen
- **anaphylactic shock** *N* — severe and sometimes fatal allergic reaction to a foreign substance such as bee venom
- **quarantine** *N* — a strict isolation im- posed to prevent the spread of disease.

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **sneeze** *V* — to expel air and nasal secretions from the nose involuntarily
- **hayfever** *N* — an allergic reaction to pollen, dust
- **reaction** *N* — action in response to some influence
- **contagious** *A* — capable of being trans- mitted by bodily con- tact with an infected person or object
