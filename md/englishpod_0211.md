# The Weekend - Playing Board Games

## Key Vocabulary

- **such a** *General Adverb* — so extreme a degree
- **cheat** *principle verb, present progressive* — to deceive by trickery
- **my turn** *common noun, singular* — my opportunity to do something
- **jail can I** *common noun, singular* — place where prisoners are kept used to ask for permis- sions
- **banker** *common noun, singular* — the player in charge of the bank in gambling games

- **Teller** *singular* — the bank
