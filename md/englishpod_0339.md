## Key Vocabulary

- **teacher** *N* — a person who teaches or instructs
- **conference** *N* — a formal meeting where many people talk about ideas
- **insight** *N* — an instance of appre- hending the true nature of a thing
- **struggle** *V* — try very hard to do something that is diffi- cult
- **compliment** *N* — an expression of praise
- **tutor** *N* — a person employed to instruct another in some branch or branches of learning, esp. a private instruc- tor.
- **tution** *N* — the charge or fee for in- struction
- **overdue** *A* — past due
