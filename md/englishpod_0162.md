## Key Vocabulary

- **armistice** *common noun, singular* — an agreement to stop a war
- **treaty** *common noun, singular* — agreement
- **commemorate** *principle verb, infinitive* — memorize
- **flunk** *principle verb, past simple* — fail in exams

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **poppy** *common noun, plural* — a red flower with a black center that grew on the graves of sol- diers who died in WWI, worn to commemorate the dead in Common- wealth countries
- **to pay your respects** *phrase* — to show respect to someone you are grate- ful to
- **a moment of silence** *phrase* — the time in a Veteran’s Day ceremony when ev- eryone is quiet for one minute as they pay their respects
- **salute The Last Post** *common noun, singular* — to pay respect to or to honor by an outward act the name of a fa- mous salute, a trum- pet solo played at most Remembrance Day cer- emonies in Common- wealth countries
