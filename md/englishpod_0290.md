# The Weekend - Buying Jewelery

## Key Vocabulary

- **anniversary** *N* — The date on which a ro- mance began
- **have in mind** *P* — to be thinking of some- thing
- **heartshaped** *A* — something in the shape of a heart
- **pendant** *N* — a piece of jewelry that hangs on a chain
- **gemstone** *N* — a stone that can be used in jewelry
- **amethyst** *N* — a clear purple or bluish- purple stone
- **bracelet** *N* — a piece of jewelry worn on the wrist
- **18K** *A* — contain 75% gold
- **blue topaz** *N* — a blue precious stone
- **platinum** *N* — a heavy, silver-colored metal
- **locket** *N* — A small ornamental case
- **pop the question** *P* — ask a big question (pro- posal)

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **blingbling** *N* — slang: a term used to describe shiny jewelry, usually with a lot of di- amonds
- **carat** *N* — a unit of weight in gem- stones
- **flashy** *A* — eye-catching, shiny and brilliant; ostentatious, attention-grabbing but not classy
- **showstopper** *N* — something that is so in- teresting as to figura- tively ”stop the show”
- **golddigger** *N* — someone who marries for money
