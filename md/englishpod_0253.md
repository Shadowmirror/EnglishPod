# The Office - Interview Skills 7 - Describing Ones

## Key Vocabulary

- **weakness** *N* — a personal defect or fail- ing
- **to pinpoint** *V* — to locate or describe ex- actly or precisely
- **go someone’s way** *P* — proceed according to one’s plans or wishes
- **get someone down** *P* — to exhaust, discourage, or depress someone
- **to shirk** *V* — to avoid or neglect
- **impression** *N* — the feelings one has af- ter a specific event
- **frustrated** *A* — upset

## Supplementary Vocabulary

- **honest** *A* — to tell the truth
- **workaholic** *N* — a person who is ob- sessed with their work
- **professional** *N* — to still behave accord- ingly even if unhappy
- **overachiever** *N* — to strive to be the best
- **temper tantrum** *N* — to throw a fit if things do no go your way
